## Accepted Core repairs

### Repair enemy Substitute and Minimize when leaving STATS

**CAT-071.** Opening FIGHT in Red and Blue selects the player side for the move-info box. Canceling FIGHT can leave that selection in place. The battle party STATS exit reads the enemy's Substitute or Minimize state, then calls a drawing routine that uses the leftover side. That can overwrite the player's sprite and leave the enemy's slot showing the wrong image. Stock Yellow sets the enemy side at reload. Shin Lite and PureRGB implement the same repair intent. [Red/Blue move info](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/core.asm#L2848-L2870) · [Red/Blue STATS exit](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/core.asm#L2365-L2395) · [Yellow correction](https://github.com/pret/pokeyellow/blob/e89ead154b9968aa50eed9328ff2b38b6c194382/engine/battle/core.asm#L2475-L2501) · [Shin Lite correction](https://github.com/jojobear13/shinpokered/blob/b35c2a536a1605841c8fb662aad18b0dbfd2a900/engine/battle/core.asm#L2725-L2755) · [PureRGB correction](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/battle/moved_battle_code.asm#L13-L38)

**Observed and proposed.** The stock Red/Blue FIGHT → B → Pokémon → STATS route changed both pictures for both effects. The same route left both intact on stock Yellow. Set the render target to the enemy for this reload. Check the player's picture and both combatants' flags, including an enemy with both effects, a plain sprite, and link battle viewing on either side. This changes the way the existing state is drawn and preserves the Acid Armor decision. [Stock tests](donor-survey-stock-checks.json)

### Keep the ghost disguise when leaving STATS

**CAT-021.** Pokémon Tower's wild battle setup loads an unidentified ghost image when the player lacks the Silph Scope. The STATS return path instead reloads the actual species picture. In all four tested Red/Blue/Yellow mode combinations, the resulting tiles matched normal Gastly while the battle name remained GHOST. Yellow's unrelated Substitute and Minimize repair leaves this identity leak. Shin Lite and PureRGB both check for the ghost image when restoring the sprite. [Red/Blue battle setup](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/core.asm#L6695-L6727) · [Shin and its reload guard](https://github.com/jojobear13/shinpokered/blob/b35c2a536a1605841c8fb662aad18b0dbfd2a900/engine/battle/core.asm#L2735-L2755) · [PureRGB reload](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/battle/moved_battle_code.asm#L15-L38) · [Stock tests](donor-survey-stock-checks.json)

**Scope.** Restore the picture the encounter is supposed to show. Check unscoped and scoped wild encounters, ordinary battles, and the scripted Marowak reveal at the correct time. Preserve the name, Pokédex state, encounter data, capture and escape rules, and Silph Scope progression. The picture leak and the Poké Doll bypass are separate.

### Play the correct sound when replacing a move

**CAT-022.** Red/Blue request the normal swap sound in the current audio bank, the group of sound data active at that point. During a battle level-up this can be the battle bank. Yellow selects the bank containing the swap sound, plays it, and restores the previous bank. Yellow Legacy carries that code. PureRGB has a focused battle path for the same sound. [Red/Blue call](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm#L208-L220) · [Bank dispatch](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/home/audio.asm#L168-L197) · [Yellow fix](https://github.com/pret/pokeyellow/blob/e89ead154b9968aa50eed9328ff2b38b6c194382/engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm#L207-L239) · [Yellow Legacy fix](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/blob/3a5358e8c9d3d0889f38cdb39a208120fea37a31/engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm#L207-L239) · [PureRGB fix](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm#L296-L318)

**Scope.** Play the existing sound and resume the active music correctly. Check replacement after winning a wild or trainer battle, replacement outside battle with a TM, declining the move, and filling an empty move slot. Preserve text, moves, and PP. The sound defect and fix path were checked in source. A listening check belongs with the patch.

### Skip the SHIFT prompt when every reserve has fainted

Red and Blue check total party size before asking whether to switch ahead of the trainer's next Pokémon. That count still includes fainted Pokémon. The later selection refuses them. PureRGB adds an HP check. Stock Red and Blue both showed the prompt after a KO with one fainted reserve. Both also showed it with a healthy reserve. Count a living reserve on the player side independently of whose turn it was. Check that the prompt appears with a reserve at 1 HP and is skipped with no living reserve. Keep initial send-outs, SET and link battles, forced replacements, and the rules for a valid switch. [Red/Blue prompt and checks](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/core.asm#L1364-L1407) · [PureRGB prompt](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/battle/core.asm#L1537-L1565) · [PureRGB HP check](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/battle/move_effects/teleport.asm#L98-L125)

## Core trainer item repair

### Skip Blaine's full-HP Super Potion

**CAT-018.** Red/Blue can take the Super Potion branch from the random roll alone. The shared heal then caps HP and consumes an item use and the trainer's action, even when heal amount is zero. The fixes differ. Official Yellow limits use to below one tenth of max HP under its integer comparison. Shin Lite and PureRGB both check below half. PureRGB also raises the chance. Yellow Legacy disables this routine. [Red/Blue decision](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L382-L398) · [Item and action](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/af519899719f0754965776faac0e836a3b906e6d/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L493-L552) · [Yellow](https://github.com/pret/pokeyellow/blob/e89ead154b9968aa50eed9328ff2b38b6c194382/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L396-L407) · [Shin Lite](https://github.com/jojobear13/shinpokered/blob/b35c2a536a1605841c8fb662aad18b0dbfd2a900/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L1619-L1626) · [PureRGB](https://github.com/Vortyne/pureRGB/blob/231abdfcf52e2d1c0ef44f96d497b9b1b1369049/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L1203-L1210) · [Yellow Legacy](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/blob/3a5358e8c9d3d0889f38cdb39a208120fea37a31/engine/battle/trainer_ai.asm#L800-L808)

**Scope.** Minimal preserves the full-HP turn and item use. In Core, use a full-HP guard after the existing random roll. This lets Blaine take another action on a turn he would have spent without healing. Keep the item, amount, count, order, and chance whenever the target has lost HP. In SameBoy, the Red and Blue fixtures both reached the potion routine at 104 out of 104 HP. The stock Yellow fixture did not use a potion in 34 full-HP turns. Test full HP, one HP missing, healing around 50 missing HP, the chance boundary, and zero remaining items. In Minimal, match the original turn, item-use count, and random input.

## Test scope

The stock graphics checks ran the normal battle command and STATS menus in SameBoy 1.0.5. Tests booted unmodified English cartridges and set up disposable parties and encounters in memory. Red and Blue ran in Game Boy mode. Yellow ran in Game Boy and Game Boy Color modes. The 28 cases compare the graphics data and screen output indicators before and after STATS. Ghost checks include ordinary Gastly, Tower with the Scope, and Tower without it. Seven trainer cases used the regular battle loop to check Blaine's potion use and the between-Pokémon SHIFT prompt. Other findings rely on source or attributed author documentation. Core patches, donor builds, and real hardware still need their own checks. [Recorded outcomes](donor-survey-stock-checks.json)
