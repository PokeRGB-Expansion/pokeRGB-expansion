# What is still missing in v0.2.0?

The fixes and smaller improvements below are accepted for the listed presets, including the lower-priority alphabetical Pokédex view. They are included in v0.3.0. The original comparison and reproduction results below refer to v0.2.0. D65 also adds the Core field EXP. ALL switch. [Decision follow-up and new evidence](exp-pacing-and-move-services.md) The comparisons below use the [v0.2.0 source](https://github.com/PokeRGB-Expansion/pokeRGB-expansion/tree/v0.2.0). Cartridge checks used SameBoy 1.0.5, isolated saves or new games, and controlled starting states where needed. Some checks used Red only. No physical hardware test was part of this review.

For the next review, see the [proposals based on the released v0.3.0 games](v030-core-candidates.md).

| Enforce the Bicycle check on Cycling Road | Core only | In Core Red, the same three cases were tested at both gates. With an empty Bag, releasing Left at the refusal sent the player back. | Initialize a level-one Pokémon with valid EXP | Core only | In both Red presets a controlled level-one wild Pidgey, caught through ITEM → Master Ball, stored 16,777,162 EXP. Its Medium Slow curve gives −54 at level one, which wraps in a three-byte field. In a separate controlled knockout a seven-EXP award took that Pidgey to level 100. For Core, start it at zero so seven EXP leaves it two short of level two. Check party and direct-to-box captures, gifts, other growth rates, the level-two boundary, and valid high EXP values in authored data. Minimal preserves the leveling result because Trainer-Fly makes it reachable on the original cartridge. [Retail example](https://tasvideos.org/2945S). This matters immediately to a hack author adding a level-one encounter. The released grass and water encounter tables start at level two in all six builds. Treat repair of already-wrapped EXP in an imported save as a separate question. A large EXP value alone cannot identify corruption. | Toggle EXP. ALL in the Bag | Superseded by D65. Core includes it. Minimal preserves the carrying rule. | A player could turn sharing on or off between fights without putting the item in a PC. ## What the additional projects contribute

- **Modern Emerald** offers Classic, Modern and Custom at New Game. Classic is initially highlighted. Choosing a preset sets its defaults, including encounters. Custom exposes the individual choices. These are player selections stored with that adventure. Its README names Emerald Worped and Heart & Soul through v1.2.1 as downstream games. Its field-only party shortcut transfers well to PokéRGB. Creating player setup pages or adopting its larger Bag would be separate design decisions. [Presets and downstream projects](https://github.com/resetes12/pokeemerald/blob/826086c294284860f6004764e5593330623675c3/README.md#L17-L54) · [Preset selection code](https://github.com/resetes12/pokeemerald/blob/826086c294284860f6004764e5593330623675c3/src/tx_rac_menu.c#L2292-L2368)
- **Polished Crystal** is a released game with faithful and default builds. Crystal Inheritance and Safari Gauntlet explicitly identify Polished Crystal as their base. Its level-one EXP fix is relevant to our authors even though our roster, leveling rules and scope differ. [Release](https://github.com/Rangi42/polishedcrystal/releases/tag/v3.2.3) · [Crystal Inheritance](https://github.com/dwg-and-dogs/PLC_Polished/blob/d2debbf67cc8b693cce84d82014a937dd7e68e0b/README.md#L1-L10) · [Safari Gauntlet](https://github.com/gillkyle/pokemon-safari-gauntlet/blob/f5c36c8a38efbf166f0d42fc0a043d9552a2430a/README.md#L5-L11)
- **pokegold-retro** starts from Gold and brings in selected Gen 1 features, including presentation, fossils and a reworked EXP. ALL. It is an example of choosing the closest mechanical precedent for each feature. Its toggleable EXP. ALL illustrates why the name of a familiar item alone does not tell an author what rules they are adopting. [Project description and changes](https://github.com/JustRegularLuna/pokegold-retro/blob/4a942f648744f772f859278050b5f55a44fd71b8/README.md#L3-L54)
- **CrystalShireEngine** describes itself as an in-development Crystal base. Its core lists wider IDs, a replacement box system, modern EVs, running and a short low-HP warning. That is a different meaning of “core.” Its documentation is evidence of intended defaults and credited reuse. Proposed generation branches have a different status from shipped games. [Project scope and status](https://github.com/fellowship-of-the-roms/CrystalShireEngine/blob/ff3c90761ee9a06950b938fbe31a8cff4b62e335/README.md#L3-L47)
