# Battle flashes and low-HP sound

Keep the stock presentation in the reference games. The two author options are independent and off by default. One reduces battle flashing and the other shortens the low-HP warning. An author could expose either as a player preference. [Usability register](usability-coverage.csv)

**Reduce flashes while keeping the battle cues.** The stock BATTLE SCENE option skips the regular move animation. Ball throws have their own path, and the move handler still reaches the hit response. Wild encounter introductions also have their own flash. Preserve the entrance shape. Outside a dungeon, stock shows a double circle for an ordinary wild opponent and a single circle for one at least three levels above the first conscious party member. Reducing palette changes can preserve that cue and the difference between a hit and a miss. The Virtual Console patch template targets individual moves, including Hyper Beam. PureRGB implements reduced-flash paths for both move and encounter effects. **Bound the repeated warning.** A short-warning option can play three warning phrases when the player’s active Pokémon enters the stock red-HP range, as PureRGB does. Keep the HP display, rearm the warning after healing out of the range, and warn when a different low-HP Pokémon enters. Returning from STATS must not replay it. Stock also uses the alarm flag to skip waiting for other sounds. Track the audible repetitions separately. Check cries and item jingles while the warning is sounding and after it stops. Preserve the original wait rule. **What was checked.** Twenty direct routine fixtures ran on hash-matched retail Red and Blue in SameBoy 1.0.5 (DMG-B). With the lead at level 30, level 32 and 33 wild opponents selected the double and single circles respectively. All eight color, level and BATTLE SCENE combinations entered the same palette-flash loop with a count of three. Four move-path cases checked the option branch and common hit-response call. Eight sound cases varied the alarm flag and a busy sound channel. With the flag set, the wait returned immediately. With the flag clear, a busy channel held the wait until it cleared. These checks exercised stock decisions and timing paths. Before shipping either option, capture the actual picture or audio, exercise stock monochrome and Super Game Boy palettes with both animation settings, and compare battle results at matched rolls. A reduced-flash claim should name the scenes it covers.
