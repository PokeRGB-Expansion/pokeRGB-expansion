## Counted items and named machines

**Machine names and details.** Show number, name and INFO wherever a machine is offered or owned. Informing a purchase or one-use teaching decision is intentional. Stock can require managing space before the player can find a stored machine’s name. [D61 and tests](../docs/15-battle-information.md)

The original checkout in Red and Blue uses the machine number. In 24 stock routine checks we followed the selected purchase through the construction of its confirmation text: all nine Celadon 2F sale machines and all three prize machines in both colors. Every string contained TM plus two digits. The mart copies the item label into its price question and the prize counter inserts the item label into its Yes/No question. Neither calls the teaching prompt. An NPC may already identify a particular machine in dialogue. In the reference presets, INFO is available while browsing, before that confirmation. **Capacity displays.** Both presets show occupied places in the bag, item PC and Pokémon box. Count stacks in item lists, including duplicates. Use the configured limits and check empty, full, transfer, and final-unit removal cases. [Box chooser](box-picker-safety.md)

## Skip a switch prompt that offers no legal switch

In SHIFT style, Red/Blue count party members before asking the player to change Pokémon. A party with an active Pokémon and one fainted reserve gets the question. Choosing that reserve then fails. When nobody can enter, both presets should go straight to the trainer sending out the next Pokémon. The normal send-out text still names it before the next player decision. Preserve the prompt for a reserve with even 1 HP. Keep SET, link battles, first send-outs and forced replacements on their original paths. [Stock branch and validation](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L1364-L1443) · [Both announcements](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/data/text/text_2.asm#L912-L930)

Sixteen SameBoy 1.0.5 fixtures followed a trainer KO to the next FIGHT menu: Red and Blue, stock and a temporary build, SHIFT and SET, and a reserve with 0 or 1 HP. Stock displayed the SHIFT question at both reserve HP values. The temporary build skipped only the zero-HP SHIFT question. Both builds named the same next species and reached the same active party position, enemy position, visible species, both active HP values, first move’s PP and participant mask under each tested condition. The fixture declined the switch whenever a prompt appeared. Link play and a full party with several living choices remain implementation tests.

## The clerk and the top prize

**Minimal and Core preserve the Red/Blue coin clerk, including the existing offer and success line.** The clerk sells a nominal lot of 50 for ¥1,000. With room for fewer than 50, a sale accepted under the stock threshold fills the case to 9,999. The clerk refuses when the starting balance is already 9,990 or higher.

The previous full-lot prototype worked at the counter and broke an ordinary route at the prize exchange. From zero, 199 purchases leave 9,950 coins. Stock accepts purchase 200, credits the last 49 and brings the player to Red Porygon’s exact price of 9,999. The prototype refused that purchase. The relevant compatibility test follows the money through both counters, checks that Porygon arrives and that the redemption leaves zero coins. Preserve the original entry, cost, receipt and refusal for each color. [Stock sale](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/scripts/GameCorner.asm#L140-L201) · [Clerk dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/GameCorner.asm#L10-L39) · [Version-specific redemption and controls](game-corner-redemption.md)

The earlier investigation ran twenty stock and twenty temporary-build fixtures after the Yes/No selection. The recorded coin balances and money are valid observations of those builds. The full-lot prototype is rejected. Its checksum remains in the archived [fixture data](scope-sweep-checks.json). The separate hidden-pickup fix selects the original spill message only when coins were actually lost.

## Two useful readouts

- **Show carried quantity while buying an item (UC-67, both presets).** At the normal Mart quantity prompt, show how many units of the selected item are already in the bag. Sum matching stacks. For example, 99 Poké Balls and another stack of 3 should display 102. The item PC is separate. Stock already shows bag quantities and asks for a quantity and total price when buying. FireRed displays the bag total at this point. Preserve Red/Blue’s maximum purchase size, prices, affordability check, refusal and cancellation. Test 0, 1, 99 and 100 units, multiple stacks, a full bag and a completed purchase. [Stock purchase](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/events/pokemart.asm#L139-L185) · [Quantity box](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/home/list_menu.asm#L197-L221) · [Stack handling](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/inventory.asm#L59-L82) · [FireRed bag count](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14/src/shop.c#L909-L925)
- **Show current and maximum PP when choosing a move for Ether, Max Ether or PP Up (UC-68, both presets).** The owned Pokémon’s STATS page can show these numbers. The item selection screen draws its list of moves and skips the normal FIGHT information panel. Put the figures beside the selected move, taking them from the same party member and slot that the item affects. Respect each item’s existing availability and use rules, including a transformed active battler, empty move slots and PP Ups. Selecting a row should never alter the battle’s remembered move. Compare cancellation and the next enemy Counter against stock. The Max Ether gameplay fix has a separate Core-only record. Elixir affects all moves and needs no single-move prompt. [Item selector](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L1954-L2001) · [Move menu](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L2520-L2534) · [Menu mode and drawing](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L2581-L2600) · [Original STATS page](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/status_screen.asm#L329-L381)

The two readouts have source-backed designs. Their finished screens still need readability checks on the original display and tests that opening or cancelling them changes no item, turn, stored move history or purchase.
