# Level-one EXP, training choices and game speed

The level-one correction is accepted for Core. The other entries below record design recommendations. They do not describe features shipped in v0.2.0.

## Level one is a real Minimal compatibility case

The ordinary English Red/Blue adventure offers no native level-one encounter or gift. Across all six v0.2.0 builds, the lowest grass or water encounter level is two. The rods, starters, gifts and prize Pokémon start higher, and an in-game trade copies the offered Pokémon’s level. There is no breeding in Red/Blue. [Prize levels](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/data/events/prize_mon_levels.asm) · [NPC trade](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/events/in_game_trades.asm#L110-L148)

A player can nevertheless obtain a level-one Gengar through Trainer-Fly on the original game. A documented Red speedrun deliberately catches one, promotes it to level 100 with a small EXP award and uses it to progress. In SameBoy, a controlled level-one Gengar capture on retail English Red, retail English Blue and Minimal Red stored the three EXP bytes `FF FF CA`. In a separate knockout test on each cartridge, using that starting value, seven EXP produced level 100. The emulator check establishes the arithmetic. The recorded run establishes the route into it. [Run and author’s account](https://tasvideos.org/2945S) · [Existing Trainer-Fly decision](catalog-decisions.md#cat-066)

**Minimal keeps the original result. Core fixes initialization.** For a new level-one Medium Slow Pokémon, start at zero and require nine EXP to reach level two. Cover gifts and captures that enter the party or go straight to a box. Preserve unrelated growth values and existing saves. The correction does not require closing Trainer-Fly. If exposed to authors building on Minimal, keep the fix separate from encounter selection. [Party creation](../../engine/pokemon/add_mon.asm) · [Boxed capture](../../engine/items/item_effects.asm) · [Sweep reproduction](v020-feature-sweep.md)

## EXP. ALL in the Bag

Oak’s aide explains the effect and tells the player to store EXP. ALL in the PC when it is no longer needed. Choosing USE in the Bag does not switch the item on or off. Its presence in the Bag controls sharing. In two controlled Core Red knockouts with the same two conscious party members and one participant, carrying it awarded 345 and 115 EXP. Without it, the awards were 463 and zero. The difference in totals is ordinary integer rounding in this fixture. The item also affects stat experience. [Retail aide dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/Route15Gate2F.asm#L1-L16) · [Core reward path](../../engine/battle/core.asm) · [Test setup](../../tests/exp_all.py)

**The recommended default is the original carrying rule in both presets.** A switch still costs one Bag slot, and it gives the player a convenient substitute for a trip to town. It also lets the player direct EXP between consecutive trainers, including during a trip without PC access. The dialogue establishes that the developers knew the workflow. It leaves their balance rationale open. An author option could give the item a field-only USE action, initially on, with a clear on/off message. A disabled or deposited item would give normal awards. Keep acquisition, sale/toss protection and each preset’s formulas intact. Later games provide a deliberate toggle precedent. [X/Y developer interview](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/02/14/afterwords-unabridged-pokemon-x-and-y) · [pokegold-retro item](https://github.com/JustRegularLuna/pokegold-retro/blob/4a942f648744f772f859278050b5f55a44fd71b8/README.md#L35-L42)

## Faster play with normal music

Official Heart & Soul v2.0.6 offers a fast intro and faster battle-message pacing. SoulGold, a Heart & Soul derivative, offers separate field speeds of 1–4× and battle speeds of 1–3×. It can do extra movement or animation work between real screen refreshes while servicing music on the real clock. Move selection stays at ordinary speed, and the code suspends extra ticks in contexts such as link battles and successful capture effects. A player discussion specifically praises SoulGold for keeping the soundtrack listenable. [Heart & Soul options](https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokehns-expansion/blob/167aa6d537b109bb229c231ddce4616974c4da71/src/option_menu.c#L311-L328) · [SoulGold speed choices](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/option_menu.c#L249-L260) · [SoulGold settings](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/option_menu.c#L399-L407) · [Field updates](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/overworld.c#L1826-L1871) · [Battle updates](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/battle_main.c#L1826-L1877) · [Music timing](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/main.c#L373-L400) · [Player discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonROMhacks/comments/1vwwdk8/more_rom_hacks_need_this_feature/)

**A proposed Core option is Quick battle pacing, with Normal selected initially.** Keep retail pacing in Minimal. Start with named pauses in a send-out or battle message. Keep choices, readable outcomes, cries and jingles. Compare the same turn in frames and the same uninterrupted theme over equal real time. Check pitch, tempo, one press per choice, matched-roll outcomes and normal link timing. Red/Blue’s screen refresh interrupt also advances randomness, play time, audio and input. A global clock change would affect all four. The project has not established a general speed multiplier for Game Boy hardware. Broader field acceleration belongs in later feasibility work, including NPC movement, trainer sight, scripted walks and step counters. ## Move deletion and relearning

**A fixed-location Move Deleter fits Extended as an author option.** Celadon Hotel makes a good Kanto placement. In Core Red, a player outside the Celadon Pokémon Center could walk there with an empty Bag and no HM moves. The hotel already has three NPCs and a functioning hidden PC. If the party’s only Cut user forgets the move, the player must teach it again or bring another Cut user to reach Erika’s Gym. HM01 survives teaching and can be used again. [Hotel occupants](../../data/maps/objects/CeladonHotel.asm) · [Hidden PC](../../data/events/hidden_events.asm) · [HM reuse](../../engine/items/item_effects.asm)

Keep the service out of Minimal and default Core. The Route 14 trainer explicitly teaches HM permanence, the forget screen enforces it, and the Day Care refuses a Pokémon that knows an HM. A deleter could return a battle slot to an HM user and make it eligible for Day Care. The hotel gives players a safe place to leave on foot. The service still changes those team-building decisions. FireRed/LeafGreen’s Fuchsia NPC provides a model for an author who wants the service. Confirm the Pokémon and move, refuse the last move, and remove the corresponding PP and PP Ups. [Route 14 dialogue](../../text/Route14.asm) · [Forget screen](../../engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm) · [Day Care](../../scripts/Daycare.asm) · [FireRed’s Fuchsia deleter](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/FuchsiaCity_House3/scripts.inc#L12-L36)

**Record the party-menu Move Reminder for Extended and defer implementation.** “Moves this individual once knew” requires persistent history that an existing save cannot fully supply. “Moves this species could have learned by this level” can come from its learnset and can include declined moves. Decide separately whether prior evolutions and spent TMs qualify. A free reminder offering every compatible TM would bypass one-use items. FireRed keeps its paid Two Island reminder separate from its deleter.
