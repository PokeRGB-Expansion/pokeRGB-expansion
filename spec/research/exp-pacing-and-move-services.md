# Level-one EXP, training choices and game speed

Core v0.3.0 includes the level-one correction, a field EXP. ALL switch, optional Quick battle pacing and a Fuchsia Move Deleter. Minimal keeps the original rules for these four choices. The Party-menu Move Reminder is deferred to Extended.

## Level one is a real Minimal compatibility case

The ordinary English Red/Blue adventure offers no native level-one encounter or gift. Across all six v0.2.0 builds, the lowest grass or water encounter level is two. The rods, starters, gifts and prize Pokémon start higher, and an in-game trade copies the offered Pokémon’s level. There is no breeding in Red/Blue. [Prize levels](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/data/events/prize_mon_levels.asm) · [NPC trade](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/events/in_game_trades.asm#L110-L148)

A player can nevertheless obtain a level-one Gengar through Trainer-Fly on the original game. A documented Red speedrun deliberately catches one, promotes it to level 100 with a small EXP award and uses it to progress. In SameBoy, a controlled level-one Gengar capture on retail English Red, retail English Blue and Minimal Red stored the three EXP bytes `FF FF CA`. In a separate knockout test on each cartridge, using that starting value, seven EXP produced level 100. The emulator check establishes the arithmetic. The recorded run establishes the route into it. [Run and author’s account](https://tasvideos.org/2945S) · [Existing Trainer-Fly decision](catalog-decisions.md#cat-066)

**Minimal keeps the original result. Core fixes initialization.** For a new level-one Medium Slow Pokémon, start at zero and require nine EXP to reach level two. Cover gifts and captures that enter the party or go straight to a box. Preserve unrelated growth values and existing saves. The correction does not require closing Trainer-Fly. If exposed to authors building on Minimal, keep the fix separate from encounter selection. ## EXP. ALL in the Bag

In retail Red/Blue and Minimal, Oak’s aide explains how EXP. ALL works and suggests storing it in the PC to stop sharing. Core v0.2.0 also based sharing on whether the player carried it. In two controlled Core Red knockouts with the same two conscious party members and one participant, carrying it awarded 345 and 115 EXP. Without it, the awards were 463 and zero. The difference in totals is ordinary integer rounding in this fixture. The item also affects stat experience. [Retail aide dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/Route15Gate2F.asm#L1-L16) · [Core reward path](../../engine/battle/core.asm) · [Test setup](../../tests/exp_all.py)

**Core lets the player choose USE in the field.** The item starts on, occupies its Bag slot in either state, and gives no shared awards while deposited. The player can direct EXP between consecutive battles. That is a deliberate Core training convenience. The aide explains the switch. Minimal preserves the original advice and PC requirement. The original rationale for treating this as an author option was the cost of the trip to town. Later games provide a deliberate toggle precedent. [X/Y developer interview](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/02/14/afterwords-unabridged-pokemon-x-and-y) · [pokegold-retro item](https://github.com/JustRegularLuna/pokegold-retro/blob/4a942f648744f772f859278050b5f55a44fd71b8/README.md#L35-L42)

**What FireRed tells the player.** The Route 15 aide introduces EXP. SHARE as a held item and says its holder can gain EXP without battling. He gives the same explanation on a return visit. Taking the item back stops its held effect. FireRed has no Bag switch for it. Core v0.3.0 introduced the field USE instruction. In v0.4.0 the aide also explains why it helps: a teammate that sat out can gain a share if it has not fainted. This replaces the old claim that sharing reduces EXP for *each* Pokémon. [FireRed dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/Route15_WestEntrance_2F/text.inc#L23-L28) · [FireRed distribution](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/battle_script_commands.c#L3150-L3184) · [Current Route 15 text](../../text/Route15Gate2F.asm)

## Faster play with normal music

Official Heart & Soul v2.0.6 offers a fast intro and faster battle-message pacing. SoulGold, a Heart & Soul derivative, offers separate field speeds of 1–4× and battle speeds of 1–3×. It can do extra movement or animation work between real screen refreshes while servicing music on the real clock. Move selection stays at ordinary speed, and the code suspends extra ticks in contexts such as link battles and successful capture effects. A player discussion specifically praises SoulGold for keeping the soundtrack listenable. [Heart & Soul options](https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokehns-expansion/blob/167aa6d537b109bb229c231ddce4616974c4da71/src/option_menu.c#L311-L328) · [SoulGold speed choices](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/option_menu.c#L249-L260) · [SoulGold settings](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/option_menu.c#L399-L407) · [Field updates](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/overworld.c#L1826-L1871) · [Battle updates](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/battle_main.c#L1826-L1877) · [Music timing](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold/blob/e73803309e78231142160b3e1029df884bc8b5f1/src/main.c#L373-L400) · [Player discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonROMhacks/comments/1vwwdk8/more_rom_hacks_need_this_feature/)

**Core’s OPTIONS menu offers Normal and Quick battle pace.** Normal is the default. Quick shortens selected presentation waits in ordinary battles. Input, audio and the game clock keep their usual update rate. Minimal, link battles, catching demonstrations and Safari encounters keep their original pace. Red/Blue’s screen refresh interrupt also advances randomness, play time, audio and input. A global clock change would affect all four. The project has not established a general speed multiplier for Game Boy hardware. Broader field acceleration belongs in later feasibility work, including NPC movement, trainer sight, scripted walks and step counters. ## Move deletion and relearning

**Core uses the Kanto remake’s Fuchsia location.** A small house opens between the Pokémon Center and Bill’s family. The service confirms the selected Pokémon and move, refuses the last move and removes the corresponding PP and PP Ups. HM machines remain reusable. Celadon Hotel was also tested as a possible location: the street route from the local Pokémon Center is walkable with an empty Bag and no HM moves. The hotel already contains three NPCs and a hidden PC. [Hotel occupants](../../data/maps/objects/CeladonHotel.asm) · [Hidden PC](../../data/events/hidden_events.asm) · [HM reuse](../../engine/items/item_effects.asm)

Minimal retains the Route 14 trainer’s warning and the original HM commitment. Core adapts the remake’s wording that HM moves are hard to forget. The NPC can free a battle slot and can make a Pokémon eligible for Day Care once all its HMs are gone. This is a deliberate departure from Red/Blue, supported by the official Kanto remake. [Route 14 dialogue](../../text/Route14.asm) · [Forget screen](../../engine/pokemon/learn_move.asm) · [Day Care](../../scripts/Daycare.asm) · [FireRed’s Fuchsia deleter](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/FuchsiaCity_House3/scripts.inc#L12-L36)

**Record the party-menu Move Reminder for Extended and defer implementation.** “Moves this individual once knew” requires persistent history that an existing save cannot fully supply. “Moves this species could have learned by this level” can come from its learnset and can include declined moves. Decide separately whether prior evolutions and spent TMs qualify. A free reminder offering every compatible TM would bypass one-use items. FireRed keeps its paid Two Island reminder separate from its deleter.
