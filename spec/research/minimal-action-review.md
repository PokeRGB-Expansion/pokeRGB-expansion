# Which shortcuts belong in Minimal?

The [recorded checks](minimal-and-storage-checks.json) include the menu-only and full-battle Counter cases and received-move cases for Pokémon traded between games.

**Minimal can change how the player gives a command. It preserves what makes a team, a route or a strategy work.** Compare the original command and the proposed shortcut from the same point in the adventure. Check what the player must own, know, spend and risk. Check what the opponent can do in response. A shorter input sequence can belong in Minimal when those choices and consequences match.

For example, a player standing at a cuttable tree with the Cascade Badge and a party member that knows Cut can already remove the tree. Pressing A, then confirming, should reach that same action. Misty explains cutting bushes and the Warden explains moving boulders. The prompt can remind the player of those uses once they qualify. Keep the original party commands, the occupied move slot and all field restrictions. The existing Surf tests include an eligible fainted Pokémon with zero PP. ## Decisions for the existing feature list

| Feature | Placement and boundary |
|---|---|
| A-button Cut, Surf and Strength | Minimal and Core. Offer the action only for an eligible party and target. Keep existing signs, people and map scripts accessible. Asking or declining costs nothing. Fly and Flash retain their menu commands. |
| Registered Bicycle | Minimal and Core. SELECT uses the same Bicycle in the bag, on the same maps and under the same riding restrictions. A stored or absent Bicycle gives no ride. Registration is a saved preference and needs a safe default when reading an older save. |
| PC browsing, STATS navigation, box counts and release confirmation | Minimal and Core. At the PC, allow viewing an owned boxed Pokémon with a full party. Check capacity on actual withdrawal. Keep the selected Pokémon through STATS and show its identity before release. Confirming release still removes it. Compare full party and full box together. [Stock PC checks](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/bills_pc.asm#L256-L318) |
| Menus and buying quantities | Minimal and Core. Return to the relevant shop list after refusal, retain item-list position, let party CANCEL return to the list and allow quantity steps of ten. Displaying the current elevator floor fits this rule. Skipping a same-floor animation also requires the same exit and map state. Purchases still use the confirmed quantity, price and capacity. Outside battle, medicine retains its agreed return to the bag. [Menu cases](menu-navigation.md) |
| Box arrival order | Keep the stock order in Minimal. Core appends new boxed arrivals so existing members keep their positions. This policy changes stored order and needs separate tests from a read-only cursor improvement. [Storage order](cursor-box-and-trainers.md) |
| Battle move-swap cursor | Keep stock SELECT in Minimal pending a faithful implementation. The Core cursor repair must pass the Counter test below. Preserve actual move order, PP, Disable and link restrictions when the change concerns only the cursor. |
| EXP. ALL, Focus Energy, badge bonuses, Gust and Charizard’s Fly permission | Keep their stock rules in Minimal. Preventing EXP. ALL disposal changes what the player can permanently lose. Changing experience awards, critical odds, stats, a move type or teaching permission changes a team’s resources or options. Core’s decisions for these cases stand. [Mechanics contract](../docs/05-mechanics-contract.md) |
| Move relearning away from a tutor | Independent developer option. A level 32 Alakazam that passed up Recover at level 31 could add it before its next fight. That changes the value of the earlier decision and the effective limit of four equipped moves. A tutor with a location, cost and limited pool is a separate design. ### A cursor can affect Counter

In a controlled stock wild battle the player used Tackle. On the next turn the player opened FIGHT, highlighted Water Gun in slot three, backed out and switched. Enemy Counter failed. In the paired trial the player also pressed SELECT to mark Water Gun before backing out. The game redrew the menu at slot one, Tackle. Counter then hit the incoming Pokémon for 38 HP in Red and 34 HP in Blue, twice the preceding Tackle damage of 19 and 17 respectively. No swap completed and the selected party member was the same.

Four complete SameBoy 1.0.5 battle fixtures covered both branches in hash-checked English Red and Blue. The first slot contained Tackle and the opponent had Counter for the switch turn. Four separate checks stopped after B and inspected the last highlighted move, its power and its type. Highlighting a move writes values read by Counter even when the player never selects an attack. A cursor-only patch must preserve the results above and the effects of deliberate navigation. Its graphical appearance alone cannot establish equivalence. ### Teaching a move and using a known move

The teaching menu checks species compatibility. The party’s field menu reads the moves the selected Pokémon actually knows. The link trade path copies the received party record, including its moves. Preserve this distinction in every preset. For example, Minimal refuses to teach Charizard Fly locally. A Charizard that already knows Fly through a supported trade keeps it and can use the ordinary field command when the other prerequisites hold. Six stock checks exercised the receiving routine and field-menu builder in Red, Blue and Yellow. A seeded incoming Charizard with Fly and Cut offered both moves, even with Fly at zero PP. The control with Tackle and Cut offered only Cut. Only Yellow’s local teaching check allowed HM02. These fixtures began with the received party record. Actual cable exchange and field travel have separate tests.
