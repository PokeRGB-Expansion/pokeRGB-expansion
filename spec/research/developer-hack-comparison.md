# What developers choose in Gen 1 hacks

## Decisions visible in released games

All four project families below ship a 2× Ghost attack against Psychic. They disagree about individual move types. This is evidence for making common edits easy to select. These projects share code, have different design goals, and form a small sample. The original games and their documentation provide the evidence for a default. See the [mechanics contract](../docs/05-mechanics-contract.md).

| Released game or branch | Ghost → Psychic | Karate Chop | Gust | Sand-Attack | Charizard and HM02 Fly |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solus RGB 1.5 | 2× | Fighting | Flying | Ground | Yes. The author explicitly adopted Yellow learnsets. |
| Yellow Legacy 1.0.10 | 2× | Fighting, with 95 nominal accuracy | Flying | Internal type BIRD, displayed as NORMAL | Yes, inherited from Yellow. |
| Shin Lite at `b35c2a5` | 2× | Normal | Normal | Normal | No. Its TM/HM flags list 51 and 54 among its HMs. Fly is 52. |
| Star Beasts 2.05, Asteroid Version and Star Beasts 3.06, Comet Version | 2× in both | Normal in both | Flying in both | Ground in both | Both replace the species roster. A file called `charizard.asm` in Comet defines a Fire/Psychic Beast. It cannot establish a Charizard learnset preference. |

## Two newly verified sign corrections

The [stock fixture results](yellow-behavior-checks.json) record the item and battle outcomes for both English versions.

**Celadon’s Guard Spec. tip belongs in Minimal and Core.** The English sign says it protects against special attacks such as Fire and Water. The Japanese Red/Green sign also describes elemental protection. The English rendering therefore reflects a claim already present in the Japanese game. Both engines actually set the same battle flag as Mist. Red/Blue checks that flag for direct stat-lowering moves. A damaging move's secondary stat drop follows a separate path. Use a concrete example in the sign, such as “GUARD SPEC. protects against moves like GROWL that lower stats.” Keep battle behavior. Gold/Silver’s item description and FireRed’s rewritten Celadon sign support this interpretation. FireRed’s five-turn effect is specific to that later game. [English sign](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/CeladonCity.asm#L130-L141) · [Japanese sign](https://github.com/Narishma-gb/pokegreen/blob/953f41b34108621b2bf13c3b1e53abfc9c3e5aec/scripts/CeladonCity.asm#L171-L182) · [Stock item](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L1614-L1621) · [Direct effects](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L5264-L5286) · [Secondary effects](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/effects.asm#L555-L576) · [Gold/Silver](https://github.com/pret/pokegold/blob/ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8/data/items/descriptions.asm#L421-L424) · [FireRed](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14/data/maps/CeladonCity/text.inc#L62-L68)

Fourteen single-player checks used the hash-verified English Red and Blue ROMs in SameBoy 1.0.5 DMG-B mode. In each game, selecting Guard Spec. from a one-item bag set the player’s Mist flag and consumed the item. In twelve further turns, a player Vaporeon used the listed move against a wild Vaporeon under otherwise matched conditions, with Splash as its reply. The enemy’s Mist bit was set or cleared before the turn. The table records the actual outcomes in each pair. The text patch still needs a legibility check on the original screen.

| Attack | Red, unprotected → protected | Blue, unprotected → protected |
|---|---|---|
| Growl | Attack fell one stage → no drop | Attack fell one stage → no drop |
| Flamethrower | 14 → 14 HP damage | 24 → 24 HP damage |
| Water Gun | 9 → 9 HP damage | 8 → 8 HP damage |

**Safari Zone East’s area heading belongs in Minimal and Core.** The sign at (5,23) on the East map calls its location CENTER AREA. Its line pointing north to Area 2 is useful. The western exits farther south lead back to the actual Center map. Japanese Red/Green and English Yellow repeat the wrong heading. FireRed calls the east map Area 1. Yellow Legacy also corrects the heading. Change only the first line to AREA 1. Verify the sign, the other maps’ labels, the two routes out of East and unchanged step counts. These two signs were listed as fixes by Yellow Legacy 1.0.10, distinct from the balance changes in that same release. ## A stock limit behind the Pokédex AREA display

Yellow Legacy 1.0.9 reports fixing a buffer overflow in its added old/good rod area display. Comet’s 3.06 FAQ acknowledges that its area display can point at places the player cannot access. These are leads about different features. An independent inspection of ordinary **Red/Blue land and Surf encounter data** found a third issue that already affects the shared base. [Yellow Legacy release](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/releases/tag/V1.0.9) · [Comet FAQ](https://github.com/StarBeasts/Star-Beasts-Comet/blob/7fa2f616979ab73525c9389e63215d67aa39758e/README.md#L104-L109)

The stock AREA routine appends the map ID once **per matching encounter-table slot**. A common species can occupy several slots on one map. The caller removes duplicates only after writing the complete list and its final byte. The scratch space declared for the list is 30 bytes. Both original ROMs produce the following counts when the linked pointer table is decoded in the same order as the game. [Writer](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L2908-L2955) · [Deduplication and display](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/town_map.asm#L373-L415) · [Scratch allocation](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/ram/wram.asm#L935-L941)

| Species | Matching slots | Bytes including terminator | Distinct maps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pidgey | 34 | 35 | 14 |
| Gastly | 32 | 33 | 5 |
| Tentacool | 30 | 31 | 3 |

**Core now bounds the AREA search.** The [cartridge tests](../../tests/pokedex_area.py) check the released fix. The original requirements follow. Collect each map once within a known capacity or stream results directly to the display. Preserve the existing land and Surf search, the order of displayed places, the Cerulean Cave exclusion and “area unknown” when appropriate. For developer-added encounters, handle the maximum supported number of distinct map markers explicitly. An assertion or bounded map representation must prevent accidental loss of a legitimate location. Minimal and Core continue to show the original land and Surf locations, including locations the player has yet to reach. They do not add fishing spots, gift locations or an area-unlock filter. Expose extra encounter sources and progress-based filtering separately for authors who want them. The evidence establishes overuse of the declared work area. An observable stock crash or incorrect marker was not established. Minimal should keep stock behavior pending a demonstration of equivalent game state for a shared implementation.

To reproduce the counts, use stock Red SHA-1 `ea9bcae617fdf159b045185467ae58b2e4a48b9a` and Blue `d7037c83e1ae5b39bde3c30787637ba1d4c48ce2` with linked symbols. Read the 248 little-endian two-byte pointers in `WildDataPointers` before its sentinel. Resolve each pointer in the table’s own ROM bank. Visit each map index even if another map shares its encounter data. For each nonzero land or water rate, count the ten level/species pairs and count unique map IDs separately. Pidgey (internal ID 0x24), Gastly (0x19) and Tentacool (0x18) are the only species above 29 entries in either game. The maximum distinct-map count in each game is 14. Regression cases should compare visible markers for all 151 species and include repeated slots, a map with both habitats, an unknown area and a larger developer map set. ## What a shared developer base can provide

| Developer need | Evidence, placement and acceptance |
|---|---|
| Party, item and map information | Solus has a player request for move information. Comet credits Shin for machine names. Yellow Legacy credits separate tutorials for item descriptions and TM names on pickup. Both presets include static INFO for machines and moves. Extra item descriptions and map sources can be separate author choices. Test a renamed move, a machine remapping and a language with a longer name. [Solus request](https://github.com/Dechrissen/poke-solus-rgb/issues/4) · [Yellow Legacy credits](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/blob/3a5358e8c9d3d0889f38cdb39a208120fea37a31/README.md#L127-L133) |
| Optional features a faithful hack might omit | Solus’s author declines running shoes for the Solus game, yet lists them among frequently requested features for build configuration. Its wishlist also includes starter selection, graphics families, separate difficulty and larger storage. Ship reusable settings and data examples for these needs. Presets can keep original movement, rosters and capacities. A wishlist records intent. It supplies no evidence that each proposed switch already works. [Author’s wishlist](https://github.com/Dechrissen/poke-solus-rgb/blob/95e157191e920162a24908cc95c358e73d294689/docs/PLANNED.md#L9-L15) · [Solus game policy](https://github.com/Dechrissen/poke-solus-rgb/blob/95e157191e920162a24908cc95c358e73d294689/docs/FEATURES.md#L473-L480) |
| Multiple moves at one level | Yellow Legacy credits Shin for this authoring feature and PureRGB separately for skipped levels. Red/Blue’s ordinary level-up routine stops after the first matching entry, including when the move is already known. No repeated numeric level was found within any individual stock learnset in the pinned table. Let authors use two moves at one level in table order. Implement alongside the later Core skipped-level repair. Stock rosters gain no new learnset entries. Test accepting or declining the first, a first move already known, four full slots, an evolution and gaining several levels. [Stock learnsets](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/data/pokemon/evos_moves.asm) · [Level-up reader](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/evos_moves.asm#L341-L381) · [Distinct donor credits](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/blob/3a5358e8c9d3d0889f38cdb39a208120fea37a31/README.md#L127-L146) |
| Full-box reminder, HM slots and solo evolution | Core reports when a successful boxed arrival fills the selected box. Minimal keeps the original notice. Test that this message changes no capture rule. Phone reminders and automatic routing are independent options. Separate automatic overflow, storage capacity and field-move slots. Solus uses a temporary additional field move and an NPC who trades back trade evolutions. Offer these as independent Extended mechanics or examples. Core’s contextual HM prompts keep the original move and badge requirements. [Full-box reminder credit](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Yellow_Legacy/blob/3a5358e8c9d3d0889f38cdb39a208120fea37a31/README.md#L125-L132) · [Solus field slots and trader](https://github.com/Dechrissen/poke-solus-rgb/blob/95e157191e920162a24908cc95c358e73d294689/docs/FEATURES.md#L368-L385) |
| Party-wide experience | Comet deliberately uses EXP. ALL as an easier, later-generation-style experience setting. A Yellow Legacy player report asks about a different payout with only one participant. Neither defines the split in Red/Blue. Keep the independently measured Core double-division repair. A modern share policy is a separate Extended rule with separate experience and stat-experience tests. Keep the English original as the default without a separately approved correction to that image. ### Reports that should stay with their donor

Yellow Legacy's S.S. Anne translation issue concerns its custom reward script. The Red/Blue Super Nerd uses a plain terminated text record. Its neutral AI scoring report concerns a producer/consumer disagreement in that hack. The [stock investigation](core-scope-review.md#follow-up-findings) checked the original pair and found them consistent. A Comet pull request corrects failed throws being turned into catches in Comet's modified capture path. Its separate Acid Armor report agrees with the independently verified stock visual defect. As of November 22, 2026, the capture change was merged and the Acid Armor change was an open pull request. Neither status makes a change part of the stock cartridge.
