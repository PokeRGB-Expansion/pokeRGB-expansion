# Day Care move learning and dialogue

Minimal and Core keep the Route 5 attendant’s original offer and pickup price. Both preserve automatic move learning. The player can lose a valuable move this way, but the game applies a consistent rule, and the Kanto remake kept it.

## What Red and Blue do

When the player picks up a Pokémon, the game checks the moves it could learn at the levels it gained. It skips moves the Pokémon already knows and fills an empty slot first. If all four slots are full, it removes **the move currently in slot one**, shifts the other three up with their remaining uses (PP), and puts the new move last. Reordering moves before deposit therefore changes which one goes first. Staying longer can put the next move at risk too. A check on both stock games took a level-30 Alakazam to 31. With four moves, it lost Dig and learned Recover. With a free slot, it kept Dig and learned Recover. [Red/Blue move learning](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/evos_moves.asm#L416-L494) · [Recorded Red and Blue checks](scope-sweep-checks.json)

The attendant offers to raise a Pokémon. At pickup he quotes the actual price before the player accepts. The fee is ¥100 plus ¥100 per level gained. His deposit speech gives neither a fee formula nor a move-loss warning. [Original dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/Daycare.asm#L1-L46) · [Price and payment](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/scripts/Daycare.asm#L103-L160)

## What the Kanto remake kept

In English FireRed and LeafGreen, the Route 5 man and Four Island woman both offer care and quote the price on pickup. Neither attendant explains move replacement or gives the price formula before deposit. Route 5 does have a refusal if the selected Pokémon is the player’s only conscious non-Egg. That dialogue concerns who can still battle. [Route 5 dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14/data/maps/Route5_PokemonDayCare/text.inc#L1-L60) · [Route 5 deposit checks](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14/data/scripts/day_care.inc#L13-L49) · [Four Island dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14/data/text/day_care.inc#L50-L127)

Both locations use the same withdrawal routine. It teaches each eligible level-up move and drops slot one when it needs space. The Two Island Move Reminder is a separate service the player seeks out and pays for. ## How far the comparison holds

- Gold/Silver and Platinum also drop the first move when learning a new one with a full set. Together with the Kanto games, these give direct examples across four generations. [Gold/Silver retrieval](https://github.com/pret/pokegold/blob/ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8/engine/pokemon/move_mon.asm#L845-L890) · [Gold/Silver move order](https://github.com/pret/pokegold/blob/ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8/engine/pokemon/evolve.asm#L520-L561) · [Platinum withdrawal](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/485c400f9d7bad10d906d556ba23e5e46d5b3e5e/src/overlay005/daycare.c#L145-L190) · [Platinum move order](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/485c400f9d7bad10d906d556ba23e5e46d5b3e5e/src/pokemon.c#L3875-L3906)
- Black 2/White 2 does include a hint that a Pokémon raised at Day Care may forget an important move. The hint gives no slot order. [English game text](https://github.com/abcboy101/poke-corpus/blob/1df697e384e7d3ef5994c011151e6820f9fd839e/corpus/Black2White2/en_script.txt#L9543-L9547)
- Sun/Moon explicitly says its Nursery holds Pokémon without raising their levels or teaching them moves. Sword/Shield adds a separate way for one Pokémon to pass an Egg Move to another of the same species with a free move slot. These facilities tell us little about how Route 5 should handle four occupied slots. [Sun/Moon dialogue](https://github.com/abcboy101/poke-corpus/blob/1df697e384e7d3ef5994c011151e6820f9fd839e/corpus/SunMoon/en_script.txt#L7192-L7194) · [Official Sword/Shield breeding guide](https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/train-raid-and-hatch-pokemon-to-breed-victories-in-pokemon-sword-and-pokemon-shield)
