# Captures, full boxes and direct transfers

The [stock capture and transfer observations](core-refinement-checks.json) accompany the tests below. The [first-use box chooser repair](box-picker-safety.md) addresses a separate stock write before commitment.

## Product decision

**Automatic box placement is an author option. Minimal and Core keep manual box selection.** When enabled, route overflow from a catch or a PC-eligible gift to the next box with room. Keep the player’s selected box, report the actual destination, and preserve save and reset behavior. Extended inherits the Core default and can enable the same feature. [Basis for the default](storage-policy-and-code.md)

This is an intentional revision to the English game's storage rule. A Route 3 trainer tells players to switch boxes when one fills. Prima's contemporary guide advises doing so before visiting the Safari Zone. A full selected box can therefore cost a player the opportunity to catch a rare encounter even with an empty box elsewhere. The records establish that the game taught this rule. They do not establish the size of its original programming team, the reason it first chose this design, or whether it considered the lost encounter a desirable challenge. Only the optional rule removes the need to prepare an empty destination box before a trip. [Route 3 dialogue](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/text/Route3.asm#L67-L78) · [Contemporary Prima guide, Route 22 storage tip](https://archive.org/details/PokemonOfficialPrimaStrategyGuide) · [Storage constants](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/constants/pokemon_data_constants.asm#L56-L61)

**MOVE POKÉMON remains a separately selectable PC feature.** A direct box-to-box transfer can be useful without assistance during capture. Automatic placement can work with the original PC menus. Gold/Silver is a particularly clear precedent: it has MOVE PKMN W/O MAIL yet its ball handler still refuses a catch when the party and selected box are full. FireRed checks consecutive boxes, wraps to the first one, and reports the destination. Yellow Legacy credits a full-box *reminder*. These are three distinct features. ## What a stock refusal actually costs

Red/Blue check the battle context, then party and active-box counts, before the catch roll. A normal box-full refusal returns to the bag. In the Safari Zone it occurs before the separate Safari Ball decrement. The Safari battle loop repeats the menu for an unsuccessful item action. Trainer battles and the old man's demonstration have their own behavior before this capacity check. The selected party and box counts are the only storage counts read here. Yellow retains the same check. [Red/Blue ball handler](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L106-L137) · [Item refusal](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L2309-L2320) · [Ordinary battle return](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L2241-L2259) · [Safari return and flee check](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/battle/core.asm#L166-L202) · [Yellow gate](https://github.com/pret/pokeyellow/blob/e89ead154b9968aa50eed9328ff2b38b6c194382/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L108-L139)

Six complete, controlled stock battle-menu fixtures in SameBoy 1.0.5 used two Master Balls, a known wild species and disposable parties. Values were set in RAM before choosing a ball through the bag. Both English Red and Blue gave the following results.

| Party / selected box before | Ball choice | Party / selected box after | Balls left | Battle result |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 6 / 20 | Refused | 6 / 20 | 2 | Battle continued and the bag reopened |
| 5 / 20 | Accepted | 6 / 20 | 1 | Pokémon joined the party |
| 6 / 19 | Accepted | 6 / 20 | 1 | Pokémon entered the selected box |

A further 24 direct executions of the stock ball gate covered eight cases in each of Red, Blue and Yellow. The four ordinary party/box combinations were 5/19, 5/20, 6/19 and 6/20. The others used 6/20 in Safari, the old man's demonstration, a trainer battle and outside battle. The routines reached their expected branches in every case. The Safari check stopped before a ball could be consumed. This second test measured routing at the gate. The first measured the actual result of a ball selection.

## Destination and commit rules

1. **Decide whether the action is allowed before changing state.** Use ordinary battle eligibility first. With room in the party, keep the existing party path even if the selected box is full. Otherwise try the selected box. If it is full, try each other box once in increasing numerical order, wrapping from Box 12 to Box 1. Include all unsaved arrivals in the counts. The search uses no gameplay random draw. If every slot is occupied, explain that the party and all boxes are full. Preserve the applicable refusal, ball count, turn, encounter, reward and payment.
2. **Use a free destination only on success.** A failed throw keeps normal ball and turn costs, escape checks and catch odds. Selecting a possible box must not write a Pokémon, change its count, mark a gift received or change which box the player is viewing. A later nickname choice uses the destination reserved for the successful arrival. Respect the configured insertion order. Core appends new arrivals under the [stable arrival-order rule](../docs/13-playthrough-intake.md#pn-022). A custom Minimal-based build can enable overflow while keeping stock insertion order. Keep the destination index consistent through naming and delivery.
3. **Keep complete records and messages together.** The species list, boxed Pokémon data, trainer ID, original trainer name and nickname must describe the same slot. Preserve legal HP, status, moves, PP, PP Ups, DVs, level and experience. Grant the appropriate Pokédex credit and any gift event once. Existing capture code sets the owned bit before it selects party versus box. Old text reads the first nickname in the box, and gift text formats the selected box number. Use the successful arrival's actual slot and box for both text fields. Keep the “Someone's PC”/Bill distinction before and after meeting Bill. [Capture registration and delivery](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L510-L567) · [Capture text](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/data/text/text_6.asm#L33-L50) · [Gift delivery and text](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/events/give_pokemon.asm#L7-L43)
4. **Respect the initiating action.** Apply overflow to ordinary boxed captures, Safari captures and gifts or prizes already routed to storage. A Game Corner prize charges coins after successful delivery. Preserve that order. An explicit deposit into the selected box still refuses if that box is full. Preserve party space requirements for trades, Day Care returns and scripts that specifically require a party member. Each caller declares its allowed destinations. Update the Route 3 advice to match the build. ## Keeping the last save intact

Only the current 1,122-byte box is active in work RAM (WRAM). The main save stores its own copy. The twelve numbered boxes occupy two battery-backed SRAM banks, six per bank, with per-box and whole-bank checksums. The PC count reader explicitly replaces the saved active-box count with its live WRAM count. A game that has never manually changed boxes treats the other boxes as logically empty. Bytes left by a different save are not evidence that those boxes belong to the new adventure. [Active box](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/ram/wram.asm#L2222-L2248) · [Save records](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/ram/sram.asm#L12-L53) · [Count reader](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/menus/save.asm#L575-L617)

Stock CHANGE BOX may initialize both numbered-box banks on its first use. On a successful selection it copies out the old live box, loads the new one and saves the game. Calling that command from battle could checkpoint unrelated progress. Writing a caught Pokémon straight to a numbered box could instead let it survive a reset that restores the unspent ball and earlier Pokédex. Core must preserve the last committed save until an existing save boundary. Never prompt or save merely because a catch overflowed. Keep the PC's selected box unchanged. [Change-box sequence](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/menus/save.asm#L358-L402) · [Current-box save and checksum](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/menus/save.asm#L219-L264) · [First-use picker safety](box-picker-safety.md)

### A storage design that fits the stock allocation on paper and at link time

One candidate is to hold **unsaved remote arrivals** separately until the next normal save. Preserve their order and destination. The resolver sees their counts immediately. Restarting or choosing Continue discards an uncommitted session. Committing a save makes the arrivals, party, bag, Pokédex, gift flags and selected box agree. At the first commit that needs the other boxes, initialize them for the current adventure before applying the staged entries. Handle manual CHANGE BOX, link or other save-producing actions via the same commit boundary. A view that loads another box must include pending records even before it saves. The stock PC count buffer shares address CD3D with battle and menu scratch, including the charge-move number. Use a resolver with documented scratch lifetime. Calling the PC-only count helper in a new battle context requires checking its live aliases. [PC count workspace](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/ram/wram.asm#L619-L632) · [Shared address](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/ram/wram.asm#L836-L851)

A capacity check can be useful before implementing this design. While the selected box remains fixed, at most **220** initially free slots lie outside it. Each incoming boxed record is 33 bytes, plus an 11-byte original trainer name and an 11-byte nickname. The stock arrival routine writes the player's trainer ID and name and zeroes all ten stat-experience bytes. Thus a *new* remote arrival can be represented exactly by the 21 other boxed-data bytes, its full 11-byte nickname, and a one-byte destination box. Store the common trainer identity once and reconstruct the species-list byte from the stored species. This 33-byte encoding is specific to new standard captures and gifts. An existing Pokémon moved by the PC, or a custom gift with a different original trainer or nonzero stat experience, needs a general record or another encoding. [Box layout](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/macros/ram.asm#L7-L25) · [Original trainer name](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L2699-L2704) · [ID, experience, DVs and PP](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/items/item_effects.asm#L2774-L2824)

An allocation-only test built both pinned Red and Blue with RGBDS 1.0.4. The stock map files report 7,646 free tail bytes across the four SRAM banks. The test reserved 220 × 33 bytes for entries and 32 bytes for sample metadata, leaving 354 tail bytes. All 21,127 pre-existing symbol names in each color kept their addresses. Both game ROMs retained their retail hashes. This establishes that the proposed reservation fits on the original MBC3 layout. It does not establish a working transaction, a final header size, protection from interrupted writes, or compatibility with other features that use the same space.

| SRAM bank | First free address | Stock free bytes | Test allocation |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | B858 | 1,960 | 59 entries |
| 1 | B524 | 2,780 | 73 entries and 32 metadata bytes |
| 2 | BA53 | 1,453 | 44 entries |
| 3 | BA53 | 1,453 | 44 entries |

To reproduce, build the [implementation revision](https://github.com/pret/pokered/tree/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a) and add four SRAM sections at those bank/address pairs, reserving the indicated byte counts. Compare both colors' complete symbol tables and ROM SHA-1 values with stock. Tie the capacity and field encoding to the configured storage schema. Allocate scratch with named sections and linker size checks. A wide-ID or larger-box build must calculate its own requirement. An implementation that safely uses less SRAM is welcome. Treat this layout as a feasibility result, not an allocation requirement for every build.

## What direct MOVE would change

The stock PC blocks a deposit of the final party member and a withdrawal into a party of six. Depositing and withdrawing move a complete boxed record and both names. Withdrawal calculates the level and battle stats from stored experience, DVs and stat experience. In three controlled round trips through the actual transfer routine, one each in English Red, Blue and Yellow, a poisoned Vaporeon retained 17 current HP, its poison and its two move PP bytes (three PP with one PP Up, and zero PP). Withdrawal recalculated maximum HP to 185 in this fixture. PC transfers therefore have game-state semantics beyond moving a list row. [PC action guards](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/bills_pc.asm#L207-L235) · [Withdraw path](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/bills_pc.asm#L256-L291) · [Record copy](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/add_mon.asm#L341-L425) · [Stat rebuild](https://github.com/pret/pokered/blob/ba9c0568f64ae602fadda577a08c66639f93082a/engine/pokemon/add_mon.asm#L487-L517)

A direct box-to-box move avoids creating a temporary party vacancy or switching the selected box via the original save prompt. A party–box swap with both sides full can be a new legal operation. This makes direct MOVE a good developer option even for original-style games; it also explains why an independent choice is meaningful. At minimum preserve full records, original HP/status/PP and names, retain the last-party-member rule, calculate party stats when entering the party, and keep cancellation lossless. Decide whether full-to-full swaps are supported in that module. The default Core can already let players inspect a full box and a boxed Pokémon's STATS without requiring a withdrawal.

## Release checks

- Exercise party counts 5/6 with selected boxes at 0/19/20. Test consecutive full boxes, multiple available destinations, Box 12 wrapping to Box 1, a free slot in the last box checked, and all 240 slots occupied. Compare ordinary catch rolls, miss outcomes and both Safari and normal ball counts. Retain scripted uncatchability, trainer actions, and the old man's demonstration.
- Capture the last easy-to-identify member into a numbered box other than the selected one, name it, then capture a second member. Check order, party and box capacity, field-by-field identity, message text, and seen/owned distinction for first and repeat captures. Test a normal gift, a full-box Lapras or fossil revival, a priced Game Corner reward, and a party-only caller. No payment or reward flag on refusal.
- Test a new game with old nonempty battery storage, a current game before its first box switch, a normally initialized save and a supported stock import. Reset before any save, after several overflow arrivals, after declining an overwrite, and after an explicit save. Verify older party, bag, boxes, Pokédex and script flags together. Interrupt each commit stage and require documented recovery or a detected incomplete save. Never accept a mixed set of old and new records as valid.
- On full-capacity tests, inspect all live and serialized counts, both nickname/OT lists, box-array terminators, bank selection on return and all applicable checksums. Exercise the storage rule with the 64-slot bag and wide IDs when those combinations are supported. Confirm that disabling rollover restores stock refusal and does not allocate its working store. Check all three English colors before releasing the feature.
