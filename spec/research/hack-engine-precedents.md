# What other Pokémon foundations teach us

An author should be able to make a new adventure, keep the Gen 1 rules they care about, and take engine fixes after the adventure ships. The projects below show different parts of that job. A shipped game shows that a particular workflow worked for its team. An announced rebuild or a project goal carries less weight.

## What authors gained and where they got stuck

**Complete FireRed Upgrade (CFRU) and Dynamic Pokémon Expansion (DPE).** CFRU compiles engine changes and inserts them into a FireRed ROM. Authors got Mega Evolution, trainer options and battle scripting in a workflow compatible with binary hacking. Pokémon Unbound served as its public demonstration. DPE separately handles species, forms and associated data. Its instructions identify a learnset configuration that can crash and explain how a standalone user must allocate space for Pokédex flags. CFRU’s save option also has dependencies that require manual edits. For Extended, let an author select a form and trainer in data. Make the build calculate storage requirements and name any incompatible settings before it produces a game. [CFRU announcement and credits](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/complete-firered-upgrade.425447/) · [CFRU features and build](https://github.com/Skeli789/Complete-Fire-Red-Upgrade/blob/b637a27898b14e25dd24d0f69a3e302f0069deb8/README.md) · [Save option](https://github.com/Skeli789/Complete-Fire-Red-Upgrade/blob/b637a27898b14e25dd24d0f69a3e302f0069deb8/src/config.h#L224-L231) · [DPE integration notes](https://github.com/Skeli789/Dynamic-Pokemon-Expansion/blob/cdfc053a56326a13dc5311b24488445e17536b7e/README.md#L3-L17)

**Pokéemerald Expansion.** Feature settings, battle tests, debugging tools, and published upgrade guidance let teams rely on a shared engine. Its documentation spells out multiplayer limits. Heart & Soul shipped its rebuild on Expansion. Radical Red’s author described maintenance costs and flexibility as reasons for a planned move from CFRU. For PokéRGB, give each release a worked upgrade from the preceding version and an explicit save/link support table. Keep roster, type chart, battle AI and battle rules separately selectable. [Expansion feature and test catalog](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/c03659025cf42a0b51c27b7df79d0dca220c3420/FEATURES.md#L14-L92) · [Compatibility and updates](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/c03659025cf42a0b51c27b7df79d0dca220c3420/README.md#L25-L44) · [Heart & Soul release](https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokehns-expansion/releases/tag/Release-v2.0.6) · [Radical Red author’s account](https://www.reddit.com/r/pokemonradicalred/comments/1uc1hof/huge_update_on_radical_red_from_yours_truly/)

The GBA projects influenced one another. DizzyEgg published an Emerald source-based battle upgrade in February 2019. Skeli announced CFRU in October 2019 and credited that work. The combined RH Hideout repository dates to October 2020. This chronology establishes specific predecessors without assigning a disputed “first.” [DizzyEgg’s announcement](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/battle-engine-upgrade.417820/) · [CFRU announcement](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/complete-firered-upgrade.425447/) · [Expansion repository record](https://api.github.com/repos/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion)

**HG Engine.** This HeartGold framework builds edited code and resources against an English HeartGold ROM. Its guide takes an author through an original species, a regional form and a Mega. Its configuration guide documents settings that need to agree across C and assembly, save-tool compatibility, and an extracted script that can survive disabling its build option. These are concrete reasons for one source of author settings and clean-build comparisons when switching options. A HeartGold Generations release also documents picking up an upstream fix for a Hall of Fame crash involving trainer graphics. Exercise an authored trainer in both the battle and the scene that follows it. [Build and features](https://github.com/BluRosie/hg-engine/blob/b578c99069f93e419071ec58a1d9399d6e1f3051/README.md) · [Adding Pokémon](https://github.com/BluRosie/hg-engine/blob/b578c99069f93e419071ec58a1d9399d6e1f3051/documentation/wiki/Adding-New-Pok%C3%A9mon.md) · [Configuration and rebuild limits](https://github.com/BluRosie/hg-engine/blob/b578c99069f93e419071ec58a1d9399d6e1f3051/CONFIG.md) · [Downstream fix](https://github.com/tamirSida/hggenerations/releases/tag/v3.0)

**Platinum Engine.** This project compiles and repacks edits against a US revision 1 Platinum ROM. Its README labels Gen 8 battle parity, expanded abilities and custom competitive parties as goals. It advises authors to make map and script edits before applying the engine. A separate project, pret/pokeplatinum, reconstructs the original game from source. For PokéRGB, an author must be able to edit the same map or trainer again after enabling a mechanic. Advertise a capability once an example can be built and played from a clean checkout. [Project goals](https://github.com/lhearachel/plat-engine/blob/00bbf5fc4d743d82e5fbbe1f97c1d8a07e404640/README.md#L5-L19) · [Author workflow](https://github.com/lhearachel/plat-engine/blob/00bbf5fc4d743d82e5fbbe1f97c1d8a07e404640/README.md#L139-L159) · [Build source](https://github.com/lhearachel/plat-engine/blob/00bbf5fc4d743d82e5fbbe1f97c1d8a07e404640/Makefile#L165-L219) · [pret/pokeplatinum](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/485c400f9d7bad10d906d556ba23e5e46d5b3e5e/README.md)

**Pokémon Essentials.** Reborn and Rejuvenation credit this RPG Maker XP fangame kit. Its editable text records identify forms by species and form number. The compiler identifies duplicate records and reports their location. Its repository setup also requires a matching full Essentials distribution. Borrow the named records, examples and clear build errors. Keep PokéRGB’s one-checkout cartridge build and native Game Boy menus. RGBDS tables can support author-friendly edits without adding a new file format. [Reborn](https://www.rebornevo.com/pr/index.html/) · [Rejuvenation](https://www.rebornevo.com/rejuvenation/) · [Essentials setup](https://github.com/Maruno17/pokemon-essentials/blob/44bb2d253f4129f9fe57c39555d38f83b8e74863/README.md#L5-L17) · [Form records](https://github.com/Maruno17/pokemon-essentials/blob/44bb2d253f4129f9fe57c39555d38f83b8e74863/PBS/pokemon_forms.txt#L3-L21) · [Compiler errors](https://github.com/Maruno17/pokemon-essentials/blob/44bb2d253f4129f9fe57c39555d38f83b8e74863/Data/Scripts/021_Compiler/002_Compiler_CompilePBS.rb#L150-L195)

**Two smaller-hardware precedents.** pokecrystal16 documents the limits and persistence costs of wider identifiers and warns authors that it rewrites its branch history. Polished Crystal ships default and faithful builds side by side. Emerald Rogue offers a complementary paired-edition model on GBA. Together these support stable release tags, a list of rules for each preset, and proof that a real project can maintain two editions of one adventure. ## What to prove in Extended

These are proposed milestones for the developer foundation. Core keeps its own release criteria.

| Author task | Acceptance check |
|---|---|
| Publish two editions of a small Kanto adventure | Share a map change, story event and reward. Give one edition a different encounter, learnset and trainer party. Build each edition from the same checkout. Play through the event, fight, reward and save/reload. Both retain ordinary catching and one Special stat. |
| Turn one feature on, off and on again | In one checkout, compare each result with a clean build of the same configuration. Cover generated tables, map scripts and graphics. Changing a display must leave the AI, type chart and storage format alone. A genuinely incompatible pair must produce an error that names both choices and gives the author a way forward. |
| Define one original species, one regional form and one battle-only Mega | Put the regional form in a wild encounter and a trainer party. Give a trainer the base species and trigger its Mega during battle. Verify the regional form persists through storage and reload, the Mega reverts after battle, and each maps to the intended Pokédex entry. Give the author a readable error for a duplicate ID or missing reference. Keep single Special as the default throughout. |
| Cross the first roster limit | Use a species that cannot fit in the old ID field. Prove its full lifecycle, measure ROM/RAM use and publish the save and link boundaries. |
| Test an edit and take an engine update | From a clean checkout, let the author start at their custom trainer, play the battle, collect a scripted reward and save. Update the sample hack by one release and repeat. Show which author edits or save changes the upgrade requires. |

The first task is a small test of the Let’s Go demake idea. An author should be able to express a familiar route with new events, teams and availability. Extra battle systems can earn support individually. A full demake, GO-style capture, all-generation content and a Special split are unnecessary to run this test.
